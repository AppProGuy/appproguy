Questions owners ask

Do I get to see the site before I decide?
We build a preview first so you can see what your new site looks like. If you're happy, you say yes and we go live within 2 weeks.
I already use Booksy, Fresha or Timely. Do I have to switch?
No. The Standard package adds a booking link to the booking app you already use.
What does the monthly fee cover?
Starter covers hosting and security, and Standard includes everything in Starter. Premium's monthly fee also includes the Google review-reply agent and 1 small update a month.
What if I want changes later?
Updates outside your package are quoted upfront based on the work, from R350/hour with a minimum of 30 minutes. On Premium, 1 small update a month is included.
Will the review-reply agent post things without asking me?
Not at first. It starts with you approving every reply. Once you trust it, it can post replies to 4 and 5 star reviews on its own. To set it up, you add App Pro Guy as a manager on your Google Business Profile.
Is the domain name included?
Your domain is billed at cost, separately from the package price.
Can I get an email address on my own domain?
Yes, for example info@yoursalon.co.za. It costs the domain cost plus R50 per user per month.
